On a budget but don't want to look like it? We get it. A custom website can be a significant investment, and for many SMEs, it's simply not an option. But that doesn't mean you have to settle for a generic, cookie-cutter website. With a little bit of creativity and a few simple tricks, you can make a pre-built website template look custom and expensive.

Here are a few tips to help you get started:

## 1. Choose the Right Template

The first step is to choose a template that is well-designed and easy to customize. Look for a template that has a clean, modern design and a flexible layout. It's also important to choose a template that is responsive, meaning it will look good on all devices, from desktops to smartphones.

## 2. Customize the Colors and Fonts

One of the easiest ways to make a template your own is to customize the colors and fonts. Choose a color palette that reflects your brand identity and a font that is easy to read and visually appealing.

> "Design is the silent ambassador of your brand." - *Paul Rand*

## 3. Use High-Quality Images

High-quality images are essential for creating a professional-looking website. Avoid using stock photos whenever possible, and instead, use your own original photos or hire a professional photographer. If you must use stock photos, choose ones that are high-quality and relevant to your brand.

## 4. Add Your Own Personal Touches

Don't be afraid to add your own personal touches to your website. This could be anything from a custom logo to a unique favicon. The more you can do to make your website your own, the more custom and expensive it will look.

## 5. Don't Be Afraid to Hire a Professional

If you're not comfortable customizing your own website, don't be afraid to hire a professional. A professional web designer can help you to create a custom look and feel for your website, even if you're starting with a pre-built template.
