An app is a big investment. It can cost tens of thousands of dollars to develop, and that's before you even factor in the ongoing costs of maintenance and marketing. So, before you jump on the app bandwagon, it's important to ask yourself: does my business really need a mobile app?

The answer, as with most things in business, is: it depends. In this brutally honest guide, we'll help you to decide if a mobile app is the right investment for your business.

## When a Mobile App Makes Sense

A mobile app can be a powerful tool for businesses that meet certain criteria. Here are a few signs that a mobile app might be a good investment for your business:

*   **You have a loyal customer base.** If you have a large number of repeat customers, a mobile app can be a great way to increase engagement and loyalty.
*   **You offer a service that is used frequently.** If your customers use your service on a regular basis, a mobile app can provide a more convenient and streamlined experience.
*   **You need to access a user's native phone features.** If you need to access a user's camera, GPS, or other native phone features, then a mobile app is a must.
*   **You want to send push notifications.** Push notifications can be a great way to stay in touch with your customers and promote your products or services.

> "A mobile app is a great way to build a deeper relationship with your customers. But it's not a silver bullet. You need to have a clear strategy for how you're going to use it to drive business results." - *Fred Wilson, Venture Capitalist*

## When a Mobile-Responsive Website is Enough

For many businesses, a mobile-responsive website is all that's needed. A mobile-responsive website is a website that is designed to look good on all devices, from desktops to smartphones.

Here are a few signs that a mobile-responsive website might be enough for your business:

*   **You have a limited budget.** A mobile-responsive website is significantly cheaper to develop than a mobile app.
*   **You don't need to access a user's native phone features.** If you don't need to access a user's camera, GPS, or other native phone features, then a mobile-responsive website is all you need.
*   **You don't need to send push notifications.** If you don't need to send push notifications, then a mobile-responsive website is all you need.

## The Best of Both Worlds: Progressive Web Apps (PWAs)

A Progressive Web App (PWA) is a type of web application that combines the best of both worlds: the reach of the web with the functionality of a mobile app. PWAs can be installed on a user's home screen, they can be used offline, and they can even send push notifications.

PWAs are a great option for businesses that want the functionality of a mobile app without the high cost of development.
